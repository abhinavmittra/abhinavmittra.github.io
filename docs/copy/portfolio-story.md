# Writing

Edit profile, project, station and story content in `content/portfolio.ts`. Keep one project record and reuse it across cards, tours and detail panels.

Use first person and describe the problem, the contribution and what remains in progress. Both agent projects are ongoing; label fictional walkthroughs clearly. Do not turn draft résumé metrics, technologies under study or roadmap items into outcomes or shipped work. Keep the 29-country figure tied to the supplied list.
